Past Performance

ICF Incorporated

Role: Lead Community Development Specialist

Andelyn served at ICF Incorporated, one of HUD's premier federal technical assistance prime contractors, as a Lead Community Development Specialist covering the full spectrum of HUD CPD programs. Her work included:

  • Section 3: Delivered 15+ training sessions nationally (2021 – 2025) to CPD grantees and subrecipient organizations, including at NACCED (2023), served as an office hours trainer responding to complex compliance questions in real time, and moderated a national virtual Section 3 compliance conference.
  • HOME and HOME-ARP: Coordinated delivery of 17 HOME-ARP training sessions nationally over a two-week period while simultaneously delivering that training herself in Atlanta, Chicago, and Puerto Rico (2023). Delivered HOME-ARP Rental Housing Compliance training at NCSHA (2021 & 2022) and HOME Investment Partnerships Program training at the Oklahoma Housing Conference (2024). Created HOME-ARP guidance for elected officials featured on HUD Exchange and made available to all 651 HOME participating jurisdictions nationally.
  • CDBG, CDBG-CV, and CDBG-DR: Developed the CDBG-CV training curriculum adopted by all HUD TA firms and coordinated more than 10 in-person CDBG-CV training sessions nationally, including delivering that training at the Newark, NJ HUD Field Office (2024). Delivered CDBG Basics training to the State of Ohio (2022 & 2024) and to the State of Kentucky (2021 & 2023). In 2025, Andelyn led development of the program administration plan for Guam's $500M CDBG-DR grant.
  • IDIS: Built the IDIS training curriculum from scratch for the State of Nevada and delivered it onsite (2023 & 2024).
  • Environmental Review: Served as named trainer and subject matter expert for CPD programs and Environmental Review in HUD's PRO Housing Launch Webinar Series, delivering compliance guidance to FY2023 PRO Housing grantees nationwide.
  • Davis-Bacon: Delivered Davis-Bacon and Related Acts compliance training for the State of South Carolina (2022).
  • Moving to Work: Managed MTW cohort onboarding and peer learning communities for HUD's Office of Policy Development and Research, overseeing an $800,000 program budget.
Featured on HUD Exchange

State of Nevada

Role: HOME Compliance and IDIS Training Specialist, delivered through ICF Incorporated

The State of Nevada's HOME program underwent a week-long on-site compliance wellness assessment delivered by an ICF Incorporated team on which Andelyn participated, providing regulatory review, file analysis, gap identification, and remediation guidance for state program staff. Andelyn also created and delivered IDIS training for Nevada state staff (2023 & 2024), building the curriculum from scratch and tailoring it specifically to the state's program needs and workflows.

State-Level HOME Assessment · IDIS Training 2023 and 2024

HUD’s Office of PD&R — Moving to Work

Role: Cohort Launch Manager, delivered through ICF Incorporated

HUD's Office of Policy Development and Research contracted with ICF Incorporated to manage the launch of new Moving to Work cohorts. Andelyn served as ICF's MTW Office lead, managing the full MTW portfolio and overseeing onboarding webinars, training video development, and the MTW Colleague Connections peer learning community, a community of practice bringing together Moving to Work agencies to share implementation experiences and solutions. She managed an $800,000 program budget spanning curriculum design, peer learning infrastructure, and training delivery.

$800K+ Budget Managed · MTW Cohort Launch

Santee-Lynches Community Development Corporation

Role: Compliance Officer, Licensed Property Manager, and HUD Housing Counselor

Andelyn built her foundational compliance career at Santee-Lynches CDC, one of South Carolina's leading nonprofit affordable housing development organizations. She served as Compliance Officer overseeing all compliance functions for a portfolio of more than 100 affordable housing properties, including single-family and multifamily units funded through HOME, LIHTC, and Housing Trust Fund sources. She developed the dual HOME/LIHTC compliance framework for layered-funding units, including the checklist, file structure, and monitoring calendar the organization used going forward, and trained property managers across the portfolio on the framework. She managed a team of property managers operating throughout the state and held a South Carolina property management license throughout her tenure. She also served as a HUD-certified housing counselor and oversaw the organization's housing counseling program. Following her tenure as staff, she continued serving Santee-Lynches as an independent consultant, completing more than 15 Part 58 environmental reviews covering housing rehabilitation projects requiring CESTs.

100+ Properties · Licensed Property Manager · HUD Housing Counselor

City of Columbia, SC

Role: Compliance Officer, 2015 to 2021

Andelyn served as Compliance Officer for the City of Columbia, South Carolina's CDBG and HOME programs for six years, managing all aspects of compliance administration including subrecipient monitoring and oversight, national objectives documentation, timeliness management, contributions to Annual Action Plans and CAPERs, contributions to Consolidated Plan development, Section 3 compliance, environmental review, Davis-Bacon and federal labor standards, fair housing compliance, and HUD reporting through IDIS. She developed and led Speak Out, a quarterly public engagement series connecting council members with constituents to address impediments identified in the Analysis of Impediments to Fair Housing Choice. She worked directly with subrecipients and community organizations administering a broad range of CDBG-funded activities including infrastructure improvements, park enhancements, and small business support across the community. During her tenure, she also completed more than 40 Part 58 environmental reviews for the city's CPD-funded projects.

6 Years Direct Grantee Administration · CDBG and HOME
